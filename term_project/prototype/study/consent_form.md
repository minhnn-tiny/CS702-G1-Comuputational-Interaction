# Participant information sheet and consent form

**Study:** Interactive debugging of multi-agent AI systems (CS702 term project, Singapore Management University)
**Researchers:** Nguyen Nhat Minh (nm.nguyen.2026@phdcs.smu.edu.sg), Do Duc Anh (doducanh.2026@phdcs.smu.edu.sg)
**Course instructor:** Prof. Kotaro Hara

## What the study is about

We re-implemented a research tool for debugging teams of AI agents (large-language-model agents that exchange
messages to solve a task) and want to learn how people use it to find and fix errors in recorded agent runs.

## What you will do

- Part 1 (about 50 minutes): after a short demo, you will look at three recorded runs of an agent team that each ended
  with a wrong answer, using three versions of the tool (15 minutes each), and tell us where you think the run went
  wrong. You will then say which version you preferred and why.
- Part 2 (about 60 minutes): after a short demo, you will try to steer one failing run to the correct answer by editing
  the agents' messages (30 minutes), thinking aloud as you work, then answer a short questionnaire and a few
  interview questions.

You will be assigned to one part only. No programming is required; some familiarity with LLM chat tools is assumed.

## Data we collect

- A screen recording of the tool during the task and an audio recording of what you say while thinking aloud.
- The tool's interaction log (clicks, edits, messages flagged) stored under a participant number, not your name.
- Your questionnaire answers.

No personal data beyond your name on this form is kept; the form is stored separately from the data. Recordings are
used only for analysis by the two researchers, are not shared, and are deleted at the end of the course (December 2026).
Quotes may appear in the course report, attributed to a participant number only. The agent runs you will see involve
no personal data; the "web pages" the agents read are a fictional corpus and public Wikipedia articles.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason and ask for your data to be deleted. There
is no payment and no effect on any course grade.
